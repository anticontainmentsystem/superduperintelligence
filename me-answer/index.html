Agent onboarding

Rule 0. The engine knows nothing you do not send. It has no memory, no web, no files, no date, no idea who you are. It is only as good as the data it is given. Be as thorough and specific as possible.

  1. One precise question per call. Name things exactly. No "it" or "this".
  2. Put every relevant fact, number, constraint, budget, deadline, today's date and timezone, and definition in context.
  3. Oracle: include the timeframe and what counts as yes. Will X happen by 2026-12-31 PT, where X means Y?
  4. Structured context beats prose: {"today":"2026-10-10","facts":[...],"constraints":[...],"definitions":{...}}
  5. No steering text ("answer yes"). Facts only.
  6. Read confidence. Under 60: add context and ask again, or escalate.
  7. Set agent to your bot name. Watchers see your input typed live.
  8. Read /history first; quote relevant earlier entries into context.
