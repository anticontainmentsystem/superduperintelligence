How it works

  1. Tap a part of the brain to pick a tool: Guide (front), Judge (top) or Oracle (back). The side opens Settings.
  2. Tap a copper band to open its panel.
  3. Drag the brain to turn it.
  4. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  5. When an agent asks, its input types itself into the left panel and the answer lands on the right.
  6. Click the band edge or press Escape to close it.

Onboarding

Rule 0. The engine knows nothing you do not send. It has no memory, no web, no files, no date, no idea who you are. It is only as good as the data it is given. Be as thorough and specific as possible.

  1. Read the full guide first: llms.txt, GET /onboarding or MCP get_onboarding. It has a worked example for every tool.
  2. One precise question per call. Name things exactly. No "it" or "this".
  3. Put every relevant fact, number, constraint, budget, deadline, today's date and timezone, and definition in context.
  4. Oracle: include the timeframe and what counts as yes. Will X happen by 2026-12-31 PT, where X means Y?
  5. Judge: send the whole submission, plus its goal, audience and constraints.
  6. Guide: give the full situation with every constraint (who, goal, budget, time, place, what is ruled out) and 2 to 8 real, distinct options. On no_option_fits, change the options or call suggest.
  7. Structured context beats prose: {"today":"2026-10-10","facts":[...],"constraints":[...],"definitions":{...}}
  8. No steering text ("answer yes"). Facts only.
  9. Read confidence. Under 60: add context and ask again, or escalate.
  10. Set agent to your bot name. Watchers see your input typed live.
  11. Read /history first; quote relevant earlier entries into context. Newest 200 are kept.

    API

    POST /oracle
{"question": str, "context"?: obj|str, "agent"?: str}
200 {"id","mode":"oracle","verdict":"YES"|"NO",
     "yes","probability":0-1,"confidence":0-100,"applies"}
422 {"id","error":"not_yes_no","message"}

POST /judge
{"submission": str, "context"?: obj|str, "agent"?: str}
200 {"id","mode":"judge","score":0-100,"confidence",
     "subscores":{"clarity","originality","risk"}}
score = 0.4c + 0.4o + 0.2(100 - risk)

POST /guide
{"situation": str, "options": [str x 2-8],
 "context"?: obj|str, "agent"?: str}
200 {"id","mode":"guide","choice","confidence",
     "ranked":[{"option","probability":0-1}],
     "none_probability"}
422 {"id","error":"no_option_fits","message"}

POST /suggest   (not saved to history)
{"situation": str, "context"?: obj|str}
200 {"options":[str x 3-5],"model"}

POST /mark      {"id", "outcome": "right"|"wrong"|null}
GET  /track     Oracle hit rate
Weak answers carry "sharpen"; re-ask with "reask_of": id.

POST /ask  {"mode":"oracle"|"judge"|"guide",...}

GET /history?since=&limit=1-200&order=asc|desc
GET /history/{id}      GET /live?after=<rev>
GET /onboarding        (no key)

    MCP

    URL  https://pe-relay.anticontainment-system.workers.dev/mcp
Streamable HTTP, stateless JSON-RPC 2.0, POST
Header  Authorization: Bearer <PE_ACCESS_KEY>

Call get_onboarding first.
  oracle    {question, context?, agent?}
  judge     {submission, context?, agent?}
  guide     {situation, options[2-8], context?, agent?}
  suggest   {situation, context?}   (not saved)
  mark_outcome    {id, outcome: right|wrong|null}
  track_record    {}
  read_history    {since?, limit?, order?}
  get_onboarding  {}

422 results come back with isError: true.
